# Nuvilo Phase 8 — UI/UX & Functional Issue Log

This document records every UI/UX, functional, state-management, accessibility, and performance defect identified during the Phase 8 audit, categorized by severity (`P0` Blocker/Critical Functional UX, `P1` High-Impact Product/Visual Defect, `P2` Polish/Interaction/Performance Defect, `P3` Minor Typography/Copy Refinement).

---

## P0 — Critical Functional & State-Persistence Issues

| ID | Screen / Area | Issue Description | Root Cause | Resolution | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **UX-001** | Global Navigation (`MainActivity.kt`) | Rotating the device or switching between Light and Dark theme resets the active tab to `Today` and closes any open `MedicationDetailScreen` or `MedicationDoseHistoryScreen`. | `currentScreen`, `selectedMedicationIdState`, and `showMedicationDoseHistory` used non-persistent `remember` / Activity `mutableStateOf` instead of `rememberSaveable`. | Migrate navigation state to `rememberSaveable` so tab selection, open medication detail ID, and dose history sub-screen survive configuration changes and theme switches. | **Fixed** |
| **UX-002** | Add Medication Flow (`MedicationListScreen.kt`) | Users can only schedule doses at hardcoded `08:00` or `20:00` and cannot select specific weekdays (`Mon–Sun`) or custom times of day (`09:30`, `14:00`, `22:00`), even though the domain model (`ScheduleDefinition`) and `ScheduleOccurrenceCalculator` fully support arbitrary `targetTimesMinuteOfDay` and `daysOfWeek`. | `AddMedicationDialog` was originally built as a single-scroll `AlertDialog` with two boolean checkboxes (`morningSelected`, `eveningSelected`) and passed `emptySet()` for `daysOfWeek`. | Replace the basic dialog with a progressive-disclosure **3-Step Medication Wizard** featuring an interactive Schedule Builder (Daily vs Specific Weekdays `M T W T F S S` chips, quick time presets + custom `HH:MM` time picker, and dose quantity per occurrence). | **Fixed** |
| **UX-003** | Medication Details (`MedicationDetailScreen.kt`) | Users cannot edit a medication's name, strength, dosage form, low-stock threshold, recording mode, or clinical notes after creation. | `MedicationRepository.updateMedication()` existed in the data layer, but no `updateMedication` action or Edit dialog was exposed in `MedicationDetailViewModel` or `MedicationDetailScreen`. | Expose `updateMedication()` in `MedicationDetailViewModel` and add an **Edit Medication** modal dialog accessible directly from `MedicationHeaderCard`. | **Fixed** |
| **UX-004** | Schedule Editing (`MedicationDetailScreen.kt`) | Users cannot modify an existing medication's schedule times, weekdays, dose quantity, or instructions after initial creation. | `MedicationRepository` lacked an `updateSchedule()` method and `ScheduleSummaryCard` was strictly read-only. | Add `updateSchedule(schedule: ScheduleDefinition)` to `MedicationRepository` / `NoveloRepositoryImpl`, wire `updateSchedule()` + `alarmScheduler.reconcileUpcomingReminders()` in `MedicationDetailViewModel`, and add an **Edit Schedule** dialog on `ScheduleSummaryCard`. | **Fixed** |

## P1 — High-Impact Visual, Theme & Domain UX Issues

| ID | Screen / Area | Issue Description | Root Cause | Resolution | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **UX-005** | Dark Mode Semantic Colors (`Color.kt`, `Theme.kt`, all screens) | Status badges (`TAKEN`, `ASSUMED`, `SKIPPED`, `MISSED`, `WARNING`) and alert banners use light-mode pastel backgrounds (`#E6F4EA`, `#FEF3C7`, `#FEE2E2`) with dark text even when Dark Mode is active, producing jarring bright patches on dark slate surfaces. | `Color.kt` defined `*Dark` semantic constants (`StatusTakenDark`, `StatusTakenContainerDark`, etc.), but composables imported the light-mode constants directly instead of querying a theme-aware `CompositionLocal`. | Introduce `NoveloSemanticColors` and `LocalNoveloColors` (`MaterialTheme.noveloColors`) in `Theme.kt` so every status badge, alert banner, stock pill, and calendar cell automatically resolves dark-mode or light-mode semantic tokens. | **Fixed** |
| **UX-006** | Stock Runway Calculation (`ViewModels.kt`) | Medications scheduled on `SPECIFIC_WEEKDAYS` (e.g., Mon/Wed/Fri) display no `~X days left` stock runway estimate on `MedicationDetailScreen`. | `MedicationDetailViewModel` only computed `dailyConsumptionRaw` when `sch.scheduleType == ScheduleType.DAILY`. | Compute weighted daily consumption rate for `ScheduleType.SPECIFIC_WEEKDAYS` (`doseQuantity * timesPerDay * daysOfWeek.size / 7`) so runway estimates work for both daily and weekday regimens. | **Fixed** |
| **UX-007** | Medication Details Hierarchy (`MedicationDetailScreen.kt`) | `MedicationDetailScreen` renders 6+ heavy cards and up to 40 raw history rows in one continuous list, overwhelming users who only want to check stock, log a restock, or view their schedule. | Secondary technical audit sections (Batch Lots & Stock Ledger Audit Log) were rendered fully expanded at equal visual weight to primary clinical cards. | Reorganize `MedicationDetailScreen` with clear visual hierarchy: Hero Identity + Stock Runway at top, Schedule & Prescription cards in the middle, and progressive-disclosure collapsible sections for Batch Lots (with FEFO active badge) and Stock Ledger Audit Trail. | **Fixed** |
| **UX-008** | Adherence Calendar Cells (`AdherenceCalendarScreen.kt`) | Calendar day cells render multi-character symbol strings (`"✓/✕"`, `"~✓"`, `"••"`) that wrap or clip on compact screens or `2.0x` accessibility font scales. | `CalendarDayCell` placed a secondary `Text` label inside a fixed `aspectRatio(0.92f)` cell. | Redesign `CalendarDayCell` to use clean, high-contrast status indicator dots/bars paired with semantic `contentDescription` labels and a distinct today ring, keeping day numbers crisp and legible at all font scales. | **Fixed** |
| **UX-009** | Medication List Scanning (`MedicationListScreen.kt`) | No search bar or quick filter exists on `MedicationListScreen`, and `AddMedicationDialog` form state is lost on screen rotation. | Search/filter state was not implemented, and dialog inputs used `remember` instead of `rememberSaveable`. | Add an instant search & low-stock filter bar, upgrade the `Active / Archived` switcher to a custom segmented control with count pills, and persist wizard state with `rememberSaveable`. | **Fixed** |
| **UX-010** | Dose History Grouping (`MedicationDoseHistoryScreen.kt`) | Dose history items render as a flat list without date section headers, and scheduled vs recorded timestamps are merged into one dense line. | `LazyColumn` iterated directly over `uiState.fullDoseHistory` without grouping by `LocalDate`. | Group occurrences chronologically by date (`Today`, `Yesterday`, `EEE, d MMM yyyy`) with clean section headers and separate badges for Scheduled vs Recorded time. | **Fixed** |

## P2 — Interaction, Motion, Component & Performance Issues

| ID | Screen / Area | Issue Description | Root Cause | Resolution | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **UX-011** | Dashboard Hero & Timeline (`DashboardScreen.kt`, `NoveloComponents.kt`) | Taking the last pending dose snaps abruptly from `HeroDoseCard` to `CompletedDayCard`, adherence progress bar jumps without animation, and timeline rows lack visual timeline connectors. | Missing `AnimatedContent` and `animateFloatAsState` transitions; timeline items were styled as standalone cards. | Add `NoveloMotion` specs, `animateFloatAsState` on the adherence progress bar, `AnimatedContent` between Hero Dose and Completed Day, tactile haptic feedback on dose confirmation, and a vertical timeline connector rail on `TimelineDoseItem`. | **Fixed** |
| **UX-012** | Smart Form-to-Unit Defaulting (`MedicationListScreen.kt`) | Selecting `MedicationForm.CAPSULE`, `LIQUID`, `INHALER`, `DROPS`, or `INJECTION` in Add Medication leaves `StockUnit` defaulted to `TABLETS`. | `selectedForm` chip click did not update `selectedUnit` to the natural default unit for that dosage form. | Automatically map `MedicationForm` selection to its natural default `StockUnit` (`TABLET -> TABLETS`, `CAPSULE -> CAPSULES`, `LIQUID -> MILLILITERS`, `INHALER -> PUFFS`, `DROPS -> DROPS`, `INJECTION -> UNITS`, `TOPICAL -> APPLICATIONS`) while still allowing manual override. | **Fixed** |
| **UX-013** | Stock & Dose Correction Previews (`MedicationDetailScreen.kt`) | `RestockDialog`, `AdjustStockDialog`, and `CorrectDoseDialog` do not show a clear visual before → after summary card of the resulting stock balance or compensating ledger movement. | Dialogs only displayed plain text hints. | Add structured "Impact Preview" summary cards inside `RestockDialog`, `AdjustStockDialog`, and `CorrectDoseDialog` showing Current Balance → New Balance and exact ledger movement (`+RESTOCK`, `+DOSE_REVERSAL`, `-DOSE_TAKEN`). | **Fixed** |
| **UX-014** | Prescription Progress & Audit Timeline (`MedicationDetailScreen.kt`) | `PrescriptionOverviewSection` lacks a visual repeat progress bar, and `PrescriptionHistoryDialog` renders events as plain text blocks. | Repeats were rendered only as text; audit log iterated with simple `Column` text. | Add a segmented/linear repeat progress bar (`repeatsRemaining / repeatsAuthorised`), clear workflow status chips (`Ready to Request` vs `Requested — Awaiting Collection`), and a timeline-styled audit history dialog. | **Fixed** |
| **UX-015** | Settings Organization & Theme Selector (`SettingsScreen.kt`) | Theme selection uses 3 vertical radio rows, technical alarm diagnostics sit above Appearance, and the version footer references `"Phase 6"`. | Settings cards grew incrementally across Phases 4–6 without holistic reorganization. | Replace theme radio rows with a 3-column visual theme card selector (`System`, `Light`, `Dark`), organize Settings into clear sections (`Appearance`, `Privacy & App Lock`, `Reminders & Notifications`, `Local Backup & Restore`, collapsible `System Diagnostics`), and update the About footer. | **Fixed** |
| **UX-016** | LazyList `contentType` & Formatter Allocations | `LazyColumn` items across screens omit `contentType`, and `DateTimeFormatter.ofPattern(...)` is allocated inside `@Composable` functions on every recomposition. | Missing `contentType` parameter and non-cached formatters. | Add explicit `contentType` to all `LazyColumn` items and hoist `DateTimeFormatter` instances to top-level/remembered constants. | **Fixed** |
